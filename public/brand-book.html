Logo

The mark is a single letter, A. It does not borrow a keyboard, note or speaker. The feeling of sound comes from how the letter is built.

AAudio · Sound Design

Primary lockup: mark + letter + descriptor (Manrope Mono, tracked).

Construction on a 64-unit grid. Gold shows the centre axis, crossbar line and apex.

Why it feels like sound

  • 01

    The crossbar is a signal

    The A's horizontal stroke is replaced by two cycles of a sine wave, so the letter carries a waveform the way a cable carries signal.

  • 02

    The apex is a peak

    Two straight legs meet at a sharp transient, like a drum hit on the meter. The gold dot in construction marks that peak.

  • 03

    The legs are a stereo field

    Left and right legs are exactly mirrored around the centre axis, a quiet nod to balance, panning and symmetry in a mix.

  • 04

    The tile is a cabinet

    The rounded square (radius 14/64) echoes a monitor speaker or hardware module. Deep burgundy gives the warmth of wood and velvet.

Colour

Everything grows from one burgundy, #6B1F3A, taken from the existing website. Neighbouring hues are chosen with colour theory, then tuned so the text pairings below pass WCAG.

Colour theory behind the palette

  • Base · Burgundy 340°. Warm, deep, analogue. Used for brand surfaces, buttons, links and headings.

  • Complement · Verdigris 160°. Exactly opposite on the wheel. The cool green of oxidised copper and studio hardware. Gives waveforms, success states and data a clear counterpoint.

  • Split accent · Brass 30°. Warm metal from knobs and bezels. Small highlights only.

  • Analogous · Plum 300° and Rose 355°. Neighbours of burgundy for depth and "live" or "record" signals.

  • Neutrals tinted at 340°. Greys carry a trace of burgundy so they never look cold next to the brand colour.

Primary · Burgundy

Ten steps from 50 to 900. The dot marks the brand colour (700). Lightness steps are even, so any step swaps predictably in light and dark themes.

Neutrals · a white that rests the eyes

Pure #FFFFFF next to dark text glares and tires eyes over long reading. The page uses Paper #FBF8F6, a warm off-white with a faint rose tint. Ink is #241A1E, never pure black. Contrast stays at 16:1, far above the AAA threshold of 7:1, while the screen feels softer. Cards may use pure white only as an inner highlight.

Construction rules

Rose = outer 2px safe margin. Green = 4px live area for most shapes. Gold circle = round-shape keyline.

  • Canvas24 × 24, viewBox="0 0 24 24"
  • Stroke1.8 px, stroke="currentColor", no fill
  • Caps & joinsRound caps and round joins everywhere
  • CornersOuter radius 1–2 px on boxes, no sharp mitres
  • PaddingKeep 2 px clear on all sides
  • RhythmVertical bars at 4 px spacing echo the waveform; use the same spacing in new icons that contain bars
  • MetaphorDraw the object, not a cliché. Avoid piano keys. Prefer meters, knobs, waves, cables
  • ColourBurgundy-700 on light, burgundy-300 on dark. Brass only for one small detail per icon when needed
  • Hit areaMinimum 44 × 44 px touch target around any icon

Counterpoint

Who it is for

Audience

Sound designers, mix and mastering engineers, foley artists, game audio and podcast producers. People who work for hours in low light and judge by ear.

Brand personality

A craftsperson, not a showman. Confident, quiet, exact. It explains little and shows the work.

Voice

Short sentences. Technical words used correctly (-14 LUFS, 48 kHz). Calm verbs: listen, shape, layer, mix, render.

Imagery and layout guidance

Do

  • Use dim, warm scenes: desk lamp, brass, velvet, acoustic panels
  • Show hands on knobs, faders, cables, microphones, closely cropped
  • Let waveforms and spectrograms act as texture, in burgundy and brass
  • Keep generous space; text on paper or night-900
  • Use mono type for numbers, timecodes and file names

Don't

  • Use neon, bright gradients, club or festival imagery
  • Draw literal piano keyboards or cartoon notes as logo devices
  • Put body text over the spectrum gradient or busy waveforms
  • Use pure white backgrounds or pure black dark mode
  • Mix more than one accent colour in the same component
